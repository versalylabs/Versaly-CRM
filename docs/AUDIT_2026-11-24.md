# VersalyLabs CRM: audit, Kenya market research and recommendations

_Date: 24 Nov 2026._

**How this was done:**
- Read-only review of the code: all API routes, `lib/`, the schema, middleware and every page.
- `tsc --noEmit` passes.
- Looked at the live site at versaly-crm.vercel.app. I did not create any accounts and did not call any endpoint that changes data.
- Desk research on the Kenyan market, with sources in §5.
- Nothing has been changed in the code yet.

**Read this first:** two problems are **live in production right now** and should be fixed before anything else (§1). Each is a few lines.

## 1. Critical: fix now (before any phase)

| # | Problem | Where | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Any signed-in user can make themselves platform super-admin.** The `jwt` callback copies `session.email` from a client "update" call into the token. `requirePlatformAdmin` then trusts that email. | `lib/auth.ts:125-128`, `lib/platformAdmin.ts:16` | A trial user can post `{email:"versalylabs@gmail.com"}` to `/api/auth/session` and get `/platform`. That means every workspace, suspensions and support. | Never accept `email` from `update`. Decide platform-admin status only at sign-in, from the database, and check `token.isPlatformAdmin` on the server. |
| C2 | **Hard-coded fallback secret for invite tokens.** | `lib/invitationToken.ts:3` | If the env secret is ever missing, anyone can forge an ADMIN invite into any workspace. | Use `getAuthSecret()`, which throws when the secret is missing. |
| C3 | **Any tenant can send email and WhatsApp as VersalyLabs.** `/api/email/send`, `/api/whatsapp/send` and the workflow `SEND_EMAIL` action use the platform's own SMTP and WhatsApp credentials, to any address or number. | `email/send/route.ts:46-59`, `whatsapp/send/route.ts:31-39`, `lib/workflows.ts:77` | Spam or phishing sent under your name, and your Gmail or WhatsApp number gets banned. | Send through the workspace's own connected mailbox or number only, as the inbox already does. |
| C4 | **A tenant can hijack another tenant's inbound WhatsApp messages.** There is no unique constraint on `(channel, externalAccountId)`. | `lib/inboundWebhooks.ts:40-45` | Another workspace's customer messages get delivered to the attacker. | Add the unique index and verify ownership when an account is connected. |

## 2. Bugs and gaps found (verified in code)

### 2.1 Security and tenant isolation (high)
- **Read-only mode is barely enforced.**
  - Only proposals and workflows call `assertSubscriptionWriteAccess`.
  - Leads, tasks, calendar, outreach, inbox, email, WhatsApp, the API and settings do not check it.
  - Expired, cancelled or suspended workspaces keep full write access, so there is no reason to pay after the trial.
- **Public lead capture has no workspace.**
  - `/api/public/lead`, `/api/integrations/leads` and Meta Lead Ads create leads with `organizationId = null`.
  - `lib/leadCapture.ts:25` then assigns them to an agent from **any** workspace.
  - `/api/contact` files leads under the oldest workspace.
- **Smaller cross-tenant leaks:**
  - `leads/[id]` allows access to leads with a null organization.
  - `tasks` accepts a `leadId` or assignee from another workspace.
  - `notifications/alerts` counts across all workspaces.
  - Several routes drop the workspace filter when the user has no organization.
- **The JWT is never re-checked.** A deactivated user, or a role or plan change, does not take effect for up to 8 hours.
- **Rate limiting doesn't work on Vercel.** It is stored in memory per instance, and `/api/contact` and `/api/v1/leads` have none at all.
- **Any agent can edit pipeline stages.** There is no role check.
- **SSRF risk:**
  - IMAP/SMTP hosts and webhook URLs can point at internal addresses, and plain `http` is allowed.
  - Webhooks are delivered synchronously, up to 10 s each, inside lead creation.
- **Platform credentials can be claimed by name.** If `PLATFORM_ORG_SLUG` is unset, someone who signs up as "Versaly HQ" gets the env-level channel credentials.
- **Minimum password is 6 characters.** There is no password reset and no email verification.

### 2.2 Functional bugs
- **Inviting a team member is broken for most addresses.** The email regex is missing its backslashes (`admin/invitations/route.ts:23`), so any address containing the letter "s" is rejected. That includes `versalylabs@gmail.com`.
- **Deleting a lead returns a 500 error** if it has outreach logs or proposals, because there is no cascade. User delete fails the same way.
- **Email sync can lose mail:**
  - When more than 40 new emails arrive, the older ones are skipped for good (`emailSync.ts:97`).
  - Failed imports still advance the cursor.
  - The daily cron syncs every mailbox plus an N+1 inactive-lead job inside 60 s, so expect timeouts.
- **Plan limits are enforced only on `POST /api/leads`.** The API, public form, Meta ads and inbox "Add as lead" bypass them.
- **Custom pipeline stages are ignored** by workflows, proposals (which set WON/LOST directly), the inbox and email send. Renaming or deleting a stage silently breaks those flows.
- **Invalid dates cause 500 errors** in tasks, leads and API keys.
- **Search is case-sensitive.**
- **Dashboard and reports load whole tables** with no limit.
- **The Stripe webhook:**
  - doesn't set the plan on checkout;
  - ignores `invoice.payment_failed`;
  - has a duplicate-event race;
  - retries forever when the workspace has been deleted.

### 2.3 Fake or misleading content (legal and trust risk)
- **Billing page:**
  - Shows a hard-coded **"VISA •••• 4242"** card and a fake **"Paid" invoice INV-2026-009**. A KES workspace sees it as "$79 KES".
  - "Update card" and "Download receipt" only show an `alert()`.
- **The billing page disagrees with the landing page.** It shows **150 / 500 leads** and USD $29 / $79 / $199, plus features that don't exist ("White-label & custom domain").
- **Dashboard upgrade modal** says "Enterprise ($199/month)" and the button is only an alert.
- **The landing page lists paid-tier features that don't exist:**
  - "Automated SLA & task escalation"
  - "Custom data schemas & field validation"
  - "Export audit trails & compliance logs"
  - "Multi-currency billing"
  - "AI Churn Scoring": rule-based, with no AI model behind it
  - "99.9% Uptime SLA"
- **Testimonials and metrics are invented:** "$42M+ pipeline", "12,400+ deals", and three named people, one of whom praises features that don't exist. Under Kenya's Consumer Protection Act this is a real liability. Remove them until there are real customers.
- **The FAQ promises a one-click CSV/Excel importer.** It doesn't exist.
- **Other landing-page problems:**
  - The hero "live workspace" shows `demo@versaly.com`, and its KPIs render as "0 / KES 0.0M / 0.0%".
  - The footer says "© 2026 Versaly Technologies Inc." and lists "GDPR" (you need the Kenya Data Protection Act, not GDPR) and "End-to-End Encryption" (not true).
  - Several footer items are plain text, not links.
- **Annual billing is never applied.** Signup ignores `billing=annual`, and Stripe only has monthly prices.
- **Onboarding tasks don't match the workspace.** They say "move a sample deal", but workspaces start empty.

### 2.4 Missing pieces every SaaS needs
- Privacy policy and terms. `/privacy` currently redirects to sign-in, and signup mentions them without linking.
- Forgot/reset password and email verification.
- CSV import.
- A custom 404 page and an error page.
- A first-run onboarding wizard.
- A help centre.
- A phone number field at signup, which matters for WhatsApp-first Kenya.
- Lead email is still **required**, so WhatsApp contacts get fake emails.
- A per-workspace embeddable lead form.
- Proposal PDF, share link and accept link.
- `favicon.ico` returns 404.

### 2.5 Code health
- **Rebrand surface:**
  - About 80 "Versaly"/"Versaly CRM" strings.
  - About 470 hard-coded hex colour classes (`bg-[#1b2a4a]` and similar).
  - `globals.css` overrides colours with `!important`.
  - The `versaly-*` Tailwind tokens are defined but used **0 times**.
  - The `sky`/`cyan` palettes are overridden to render gold.
- **The logo doesn't match the app.** It is orange on dark green, while the app is navy and gold.
- **Very large pages:** calendar (1,635 lines), copilot (1,217), admin/users (906).
- **Minified one-line pages:** automation, customer-success, support, integrations, whatsapp.
- **Accessibility:**
  - 222 inputs but only 8 labels.
  - Focus outlines are removed everywhere.
  - 134 uses of 8–10 px text.
  - 11 non-existent Tailwind classes (`h-4.5`, `pt-18`).
- **Leftovers from the earlier real-estate product:** the `BusinessType` enum, `PROPERTY_VIEWING`, the `str_live_` API key prefix and "Straten" comments.
- **Database tooling:**
  - There is no `prisma migrate` baseline, and the build still runs `db push`.
  - The local `.env` points at a Neon database. Confirm it is **not** production before running `npm run dev`, because that runs `db push`.
- **Housekeeping:** 30+ phase notes sit at the repo root.

### 2.6 What's already good (keep)
- Most of the Tier 0 fixes from the 17 Nov audit are in:
  - proposals, activity, search, calendar and the inbox are filtered by workspace;
  - webhook signatures are checked;
  - channel secrets are encrypted;
  - plan limits come from one source;
  - the cron is scheduled;
  - inbound email works over IMAP.
- TypeScript compiles clean, and health reports `ready`.
- The feature surface is broad: pipeline with custom stages, automation, inbox, customer success, developer API and webhooks, and a platform console.

## 3. Kenya market: what the product should become

**Position:** _"The CRM that gets Kenyan businesses paid. WhatsApp, M-Pesa and eTIMS in one place."_

No product combines a CRM, M-Pesa auto-reconciliation, a WhatsApp inbox and eTIMS invoicing at SME prices in KES. The options today are:
- **Global CRMs** (HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive): USD and card only, no M-Pesa, no eTIMS.
- **WhatsApp tools** (WATI from $149/mo, respond.io): not a CRM.
- **Local tools:** thin feature sets.
- **HelloDuty** (Kenyan): $199–$1,999 a month.

**What Kenyan SMEs actually struggle with:**
1. **M-Pesa collections and reconciliation.** Paybill and Till payments are matched by hand from the free-text account reference.
2. **Installments and debt follow-up.** This affects schools (fees), SACCOs and microfinance, real estate (rent and off-plan), solar/PAYG, insurance premiums and car hire-purchase.
3. **WhatsApp-first sales and support.** Since 1 Oct 2026 Meta charges about KES 0.52 per service reply after the first 1,000 free each month, so a cost meter is valuable.
4. **An SMS fallback** through Africa's Talking.
5. **KRA eTIMS.** From the 2026 year of income, KRA checks declared income and expenses against eTIMS invoices, so B2B customers will demand eTIMS invoices.
6. **The Data Protection Act 2019.** Registration with the ODPC is mandatory for direct-marketing businesses. Consent capture, data export and delete, and an audit log are therefore selling points.
7. **Low-end Android phones and patchy data.** This calls for a lightweight PWA, offline field-rep mode and low data use.
8. **Field sales teams.** They need GPS check-ins and visit logs.
9. **Swahili.** A Swahili UI and templates are a cheap differentiator.

**Collecting your own subscriptions:**
- Stripe doesn't onboard Kenyan businesses directly. Kenya is served through **Paystack**, which Stripe owns: M-Pesa 1.5%, cards 2.9%.
- Paystack subscriptions are **card-only**. M-Pesa payments work only as one-off charges.
- Recommendation:
  - Switch billing to Paystack.
  - Push annual prepayment.
  - For M-Pesa payers, send an STK Push renewal prompt by WhatsApp or SMS 3 days before expiry.
- **Question for you:** is the Stripe account in `.env` registered to a non-Kenyan entity? If not, it cannot go live.

**WhatsApp for customers:** become a Meta **Tech Provider** and use **Embedded Signup**. Customers connect their own number in two clicks and **Meta bills them directly**, so you carry no per-message cost.

## 4. Pricing tiers redesigned

**What's wrong today:**
- Tiers differ only in limits. No feature is gated by plan in code.
- Several of the "premium" features don't exist.
- Against the market:
  - Growth Pro works out to about $6/user with WhatsApp, against WATI at $149/mo.
  - Enterprise works out to about $3/user.
  - Both upper tiers are **underpriced**.
  - Starter is too expensive for a one-person business. Sole traders (insurance agents, property agents) are a large segment.

**Proposal:** gate on _money features_ (M-Pesa, eTIMS, WhatsApp API, AI, automation), not just lead counts. Each tier unlocks a clear new outcome. Prices are per month in KES, billed annually; monthly billing costs about 20% more.

- **Grandfather any existing customers** at their current price.
- Whether to raise prices is your call. If you prefer, keep Growth Pro at 8,200 for launch and raise it once M-Pesa and eTIMS ship.
